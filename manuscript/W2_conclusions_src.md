Monotonicity of a two-layer fuzzy aggregation model can be guaranteed by construction at almost no cost. In the Mamdani layer, three design choices — ordered Ruspini partitions, product conjunction and bounded-sum aggregation of rules that share a consequent — make the vector of consequent strengths stochastically monotone in every input for any monotone rule base, and reduce output monotonicity to one finite-dimensional property of the consequent sets, which we certify exactly. In the Choquet layer, a change of variables turns the monotonicity constraints of a 2-additive capacity into bounds, makes penalized logistic learning convex, and recovers the optimum of choquistic regression with a standard quasi-Newton solver. Both results are checkable in practice: the certificate and the node margins of condition (C) are finite computations that can be reported alongside any fitted screening model.
